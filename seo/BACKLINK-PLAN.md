## Outreach email drafts (top 10)

**General rules:** one specific compliment, one clear ask, one easy yes, a real photo attached, no attachments that look like press releases. Follow up once after 7–10 days.

### 1. Wedding photographer partner
Subject: Feature Akshaya Milan's lawn in your portfolio?
> Hi {Name},
> I loved your {specific wedding} shoot — especially {specific detail}. I manage Akshaya Milan, a wedding venue on Kanakapura Road with an open-air lawn (up to 1200 guests) and AC halls.
> When you next shoot here, would you consider adding us to the venues you list on your site? We'll happily credit you and share the feature with our couples. Here's a quick look: https://www.akshayamilan.com/venues
> Thanks, {Your name} · Akshaya Milan · +91 98456 91995
