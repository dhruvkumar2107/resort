The Akshaya Milan Experience

Where Celebrations Become Memories.

Set in State Highway 85, Thavarekere, Bengaluru, Karnataka 562130, Akshaya Milan is home to weddings, receptions and celebrations of every scale. Mud-toned walls, hand-painted heritage details, warm lighting and open-air spaces create a setting that feels rooted, royal and deeply personal. From the first welcome to the last farewell, our team hosts every guest as family.
